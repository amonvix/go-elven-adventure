# ⚔️ Phase 02 — Forest of Decisions

## Quest 3: The Forked Judgment

The shadows inside the forest begin to change.

No longer do the trees whisper the same warnings.

Now...
they ask questions.

Travelers who enter the deepest parts of the Forest of Decisions report strange encounters.

Some hear voices offering power.
Others hear promises of glory.
And a few never return at all.

The Guild suspects the existence of an ancient magical structure hidden beneath the roots of the forest:

The Chamber of Judgment.

A place where fate itself branches into different outcomes.

The Guild Master slowly walks toward the center of the hall.

Without saying a word, he places three ancient masks upon the table.

One golden.
One silver.
One black.

Then he speaks:

> "The forest no longer asks whether you live or die.
> 
> Now...
> it asks who you truly are."

The torches dim.

And new runes ignite across the Guild Hall walls:

CHOICE
PATH
CONSEQUENCE
