# ⚔️ Phase 02 — Forest of Decisions

## Stage Boss: Decide the Hero's Fate

The Forest of Decisions grows silent.

No creatures move.
No wind blows through the ancient leaves.

Even the shadows themselves seem to wait.

At the center of the forest, beyond the Chamber of Judgment, an ancient stone throne rises from the ground.

Upon it rests a final artifact.

The Crown of Fate.

The Guild Master slowly approaches the throne, but stops before touching it.

His expression changes.

For the first time since the beginning of your journey...

he seems worried.

The old mage turns toward the heroes and speaks:

> "Every choice leaves a mark upon the world.
>
> But some choices...
> define who deserves to continue."

The throne begins to glow.

Ancient runes ignite across the forest floor.

And then...

a voice echoes through the trees:

CHOOSE.
